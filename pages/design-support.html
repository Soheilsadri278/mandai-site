Design and Planning Support

Benefit from our free design and planning service, created to help you decide which fixtures are best suited to your retail space. We fully understand how crucial the right store layout is for creating a great shopping experience.

Our team of interior designers will provide you with technical 2D and 3D drawings, allowing you to visualize your space effortlessly. With years of experience, we’ll help you plan a store that isn’t just beautiful but optimizes your space efficiently and enhances customer flow.

How it works

Explore our products

Browse our online store to get an idea for what products you like. Beyond the finishes shown online, we offer many more colors and styles. We offer a free sample service so you can decide in person.

Let´s plan

Get in touch and let us know what products you’re interested in and your store’s size. Send us measurements and any materials you have, whether it’s a floor plan, sketches or photos. Our team will work closely with you to understand your requirements.

Receive your drawings

Within a few days, you’ll receive the drawings along with an itemized quote including delivery charges. Let us know any questions, as well as adjustments and changes needed.

Place your order

Once you are happy to go ahead, just send a confirmation. Your furniture order will now be sent to production and we will notify you when your order is about to leave our premises!
