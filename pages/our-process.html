The Mandai Experience - Our Process

See what a typical retail project with Mandai Design looks like - from the first idea and planning stages to production, delivery, setup and opening day.

1: The Vision

Every successful store starts with an idea. Explore the possibilities, find the right colours and finishes for your space, and take the first step towards bringing your vision to life.

Order Free Samples

2: The Plan

Now it’s time to turn ideas into a clear plan. With complimentary expert advice, layouts and drawings, we work with you to shape your vision and make the most of your space.

Get Complimentary Design Support

3: The Production

Once the plan is finalised, your fixtures go into production. Made in-house in Germany by an experienced team, with craftsmanship, precision and attention to detail at every stage.

4: The Delivery

From our production in Germany to stores around the world. Every order is carefully prepared for its journey, with an experienced team taking care of the details of international delivery.

5: The Setup

Your delivery has arrived — and your space starts to come together. Our fixtures are designed for straightforward assembly, so you can unpack, build and start bringing your store to life.

View Assembly Videos and Instructions

6: The Opening

The empty space and first ideas have become a finished store. Opening day marks the end of the project — and the beginning of something new.
