Our Experience

Mandai Design is your online shop for shop fittings. As a medium-sized family business (Modern Collection), we have been developing, producing and selling shop fitting furniture and individual shop systems for retailers since 1996. 25 years of experience in retail, as interior designers and shopfitters have helped us to equip over 60,000 square meters of retail space. We have set up hundreds of shops - from small boutiques to department stores - with 100% customer satisfaction. With the focus on high-quality shop fittings, we offer unique shop design ideas for everything to do with shopfitting. Our flexible shopfitting systems enable you to furnish your shop in a modern and timeless style, in line with your individual ideas and needs.

Our Strategy

The rising competition in the retail sector makes it crucial to offer customers a unique shopping experience. Individual solutions are therefore required for furnishing stores. Well-designed shop fittings play an important role. In order not to lose sight of the costs, modular shop systems are becoming increasingly popular. Mandai Design offers products that can be used to easily put together an individual look for your store. And this includes all the advantages of pre-configured systems. Depending on the retail sector, individual pieces of furniture or entire systems can be put together in a tailor-made manner. Inspired by the latest international trends, you’ll receive distinctive furniture and shop fittings for the point of sale.
