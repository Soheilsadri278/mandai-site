How it works

  1. 1

    Tell us your space

    Send us your floor plan, measurements and ideas via the contact form - or visit our showroom in Geithain, Germany by prior appointment.

  2. 2

    We plan with you

    Our team of interior designers designs your shelving configuration - free of charge. Not sure about the finish? We send up to three free material samples.

  3. 3

    Made and delivered

    Your customized shelving is manufactured in our own factory in Germany and delivered to your store - flat-packed for easy self-assembly.
