A Timeless Industrial-Style Retail Shelving System

Industrial-style retail design takes inspiration from the distinctive architecture of former factory buildings. Metal surfaces, clean lines, and open constructions create a bold and authentic look. The Bergen retail shelving system embraces these design elements while softening them with the natural warmth of wood finishes. The result is a striking shopfitting feature that enhances the overall interior without distracting from the products on display.

Versatile Shelving for a Variety of Retail Concepts

The retail shelving system Bergen is suitable for a wide range of industries and interior concepts. It works particularly well in menswear stores, sporting goods stores, and coffee shops, where its industrial aesthetic naturally complements the overall interior concept. In yoga studios or shoe stores, it creates an interesting contrast and adds a unique visual identity to the space.

All shelving systems from Takinta Design are modular. As well as being easy to put together, that means they can be adapted and expanded to suit your needs. Shelves and display fittings on most systems are height-adjustable in 50 mm (1.97") increments and can be flexibly arranged. Exceptions include the Bergen shelving system and the Ontario clothing rail system. With Bergen, shelves and display fittings can be adjusted in 345 mm (13.58") increments, while their positions on Ontario are fixed.

Takinta Design shelving can therefore be extended and quickly redesigned at any time when product ranges change, new collections arrive, or even if you move premises.
