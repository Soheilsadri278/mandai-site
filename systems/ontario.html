Ontario In Action

See how quick and easy it is to assemble our Ontario wall rail system – and how versatile the final look can be. Style it with or without wooden elements, use the top glass shelf for bags, shoes or decorative pieces, and create a clean, modern display that adapts beautifully to your products and space.

Benefits of the Ontario clothing rail system

The Ontario wall-mounted clothing rail elegantly showcases clothing with its minimalist design, embodying a modern style. It seamlessly integrates into various rooms, whether against natural stone backdrops or plain walls, effortlessly complementing any decor. Additionally, it provides ample hanging space and features glass shelves for displaying accessories, bags, or decorations with convenience and style.

Space-saving solution for any size of space

When presenting clothes, whether on shelves, in open cupboards, or using clothes racks, consider the room size and overall concept. In smaller boutiques, minimalist furniture with clean lines like the Ontario wall-mounted clothes rail offers an ideal space-saving solution. Its simple design fits perfectly without overwhelming the space.

For larger areas, the modular Ontario clothes rail system allows easy expansion as needed.

Versatile uses – coat rack, wardrobe, or shop fittings

The Ontario wall-mounted clothes rail excels in retail displays and doubles as a convenient coat rack for entrances in settings like doctor’s offices, hair salons, and beauty studios.

At home, it’s perfect for entryways as a wall-mounted coat rack and serves well as an open wardrobe in bedrooms or dressing rooms. Metal clothes rails, especially in an industrial style, offer a stylish alternative to traditional wooden wardrobes.

Clothing rail system Ontario - modern shop fittings with style

Perfect use of retail space

With rising prices for rented commercial space, the desire for effectively used sales space is increasing. Ontario, as a slim and straight-lined clothes rail system, offers a good alternative to the classic shelf or cupboard.

Another advantage is the uncomplicated design. Additional elements can be added flexibly and quickly when changing the product range or expanding.

High-quality powder coating

The powder-coated steel construction is scratch-resistant and available in black or white. Both surfaces are matt and have a high-quality fine structure.

Black reinforces a classic, industrial atmosphere, while white is lighter and more feminine. Both colours are classics and both timeless and modern.

Uncomplicated assembly - DIY!

Assembly is uncomplicated and can also be done by yourself. The Ontario clothes rail stands on the floor as a complete construction and is only mounted on the wall in the upper area.

Adjustable feet on the wall uprights facilitate accurate assembly if the floor is uneven. A fastening set with screws for wall mounting and assembly instructions are included in the delivery.
