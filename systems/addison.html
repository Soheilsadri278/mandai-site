Addison shelving and shelving system

Shelving. Modules. Classics. The Addison shelving system is a modular shopfitting classic: a clear steel silhouette combined with wooden shelf boards or glass elements. All components hook in without tools and can be rearranged in 5 cm steps - so your retail space stays as flexible as your product range.

  • Modular and flexible

    Shelf boards and front arms can be attached, moved and rearranged at any time - without tools.

  • Versatile in its use

    From fashion to fine foods: Addison adapts to every retail sector and every room situation.

  • Rearrange at any time

    Everything hooks in in 5 cm steps - displays evolve as your product range changes.

  • Customizable in color and size

    Powder-coated steel, various wood decors, custom sizes and depths on request.
