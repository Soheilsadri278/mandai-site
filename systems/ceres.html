Ceres shelving and shelving system

Lightness. Flexibility. System. With the Ceres shelving system, you switch between hanging and folded presentation in a few simple steps: shelves and rails hook into the vertical wall columns without tools. An open design without a back panel creates stylish looks through to the wall.

  • Hook in without tools

    Shelves and rails hook into the wall columns - presentation changes in seconds.

  • Height-adjustable

    All elements adjust in 5 cm steps - follow your changing ranges.

  • Seamless modules

    Individual modules connect seamlessly - extend your wall as your store grows.

  • Made in Germany

    Manufactured in our own factory with powder-coated steel and wood decors.
