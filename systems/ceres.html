Versatile. Minimalist. Modern.

Less is more with Ceres, and beautifully so. This modular shopfitting and shelving collection offers a pleasingly pared-back look that allows all kinds of retail products to shine. Available in black and white, Ceres is simple yet striking.

  • Versatility built in

    Ceres comes in two heights and fixtures include shelves, rails and display boxes.

  • Modular design

    Ceres is modular so it’s incredibly easy to reconfigure, rearrange, extend and expand.

  • Built to last

    You can count on Ceres’s welded frames, high-quality materials and durable construction.

  • Customisable finishes

    Ceres is available in various materials and hundreds of different finishes. Request your free samples.

Yes! Our steel parts can be powder-coated in various RAL colours on special request and a large selection of wood decor and solid wood surfaces are available to choose from. We provide a complimentary online sample service, allowing you to select your preferred finishes and have them delivered directly to you.

Shelving systems from Mandai Design can be individually adapted to your needs thanks to their modular construction. Shelves and display fittings are height-adjustable every 5 cm / 1.97 inches (except Bergen and Glasgow) and can be easily combined with each other as desired. This means that Mandai Design shelving can be extended and quickly redesigned at any time, so that you benefit permanently, even if your product range changes or you move premises.
