Brooklyn shopfitting system - bring in industrial style into your retail space

Set cool accents in your shop and give it the flair of the good old days of a factory. Individual shop counters and matching display tables become brilliant eye-catchers. Equipped with the Brooklyn shopfitting system with glass- or wooden shelves, your shop or showroom is sure to make a lasting impression.

Client store highlight

Brooklyn shelving system - uncompromising functionality and style

In terms of practicality, the horizontally adjustable wall system knows no compromises. All standard display fittings such as shelves, support bars and front arms make this shelving system an all-rounder for selling and presenting your products. With 625 mm / 24.6 inch and 1000 mm / 39.4 inch centre-to-centre dimensions, the Brooklyn wall system can be adapted to all individual room dimensions.

Depending on the spatial conditions, the Brooklyn shopfitting system can be combined with concrete or darker wood. Unrendered wall surfaces or bricks effectively emphasise the industrial look.
