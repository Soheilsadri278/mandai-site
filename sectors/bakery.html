Stylish Bakery Fixtures that Put Your Goods Center Stage

A well-designed display enhances the presentation of your baked goods - whether it’s bread, rolls, baguettes, cakes, or pastries - helping to drive sales. Aesthetic bakery furniture allows you to highlight your goods and direct your customers’ attention to the various delicacies in your range. In addition to the positive influence on sales, Mandai Design’s shopfitting systems create a professional ambience that elevates the value of your goods.

All elements of our store fixtures are made from robust materials. The careful processing in our own factory – Made in Germany– ensures that the bakery and pastry shelves are particularly durable. Our versatile retail display shelves are characterized by their modularity and high quality.

At Mandai Design, our bakery fixtures are designed  with modularty in mind, allowing you to adapt them  to your needs. The modern designs of our retail display shelves create an inviting atmosphere for customers through clear lines and high-quality finishes. Thanks to the flexible modular systems, the furnishings can be adapted to changes in your product range in just a few uncomplicated steps.

Tasteful Fixtures for Your Bakery

Present different types of bread and baked goods in visually appealing ways through a combination of straight and slanted bread shelves. This strategic  presentation guides your customers’ attention towards different product categories. Choose from three different wood finishes online or get in touch with us to discover other materials, colors and, if required, tailor-made variants that match your store concept.

Showcase Your Products with Additional Bakery Fixtures

Add a personal touch with chalkboards: Guide your customers with creative illustrations or descriptions through your assortment of baked goods. Flexible lettering quickly draws attention to new products or promotions. This simple yet effective addition makes your bakery equipment more dynamic, lively, and inviting– for an interior design that convinces your customers even more of your baked goods.

Customized Design Options for Your Bakery Fixtures

Attractive bread baskets are best presented with suspended displays  – using back bars with fromt arms or with pedestals on the floor. Complement these with our display tables for retail stores, perfect forhighlighting additional products such as regional delicacies, homemade spreads, or seasonal specialties in an aesthetic way. Integrate bakery display tables into your bakery fixtures and create a dynamic sales area that invites your customers to discover and taste. This way, you will transform your bakery into a place full of delight and variety.

Flexible Combination of Bakery Fixtures

Making Optimal Use of the Sales Area
