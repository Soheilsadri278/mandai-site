Design Your Yoga Studio with Stylish Furniture

The atmosphere in your yoga studio contributes significantly to the well-being of your guests and the success of your courses. A well-thought-out yoga studio design creates a place that radiates peace and harmonyand is simultaneously functional. Mandai Designs’ yoga studio furniture and shopfitting systems score points with high-quality materials, natural colors, and clear lines – an excellent choice for a comfortable ambience right from the start.

Choose your yoga studio furniture to reflect your philosophy and combine aesthetics, comfort, and functionality. Thanks to their modularity, our yoga studio shelves are easily adapted to your requirements. Whether yoga blocks and straps, meditation cushions, blankets, or yoga mats – our furnishing solutions provide the right space for your yoga studio equipment. For a tidy and visually appealing environment.

Yoga Studio Furniture with Modular, Open Shelves

Our modular and open shelving solutions provide flexible storage for yoga mats, towels and other equipment, making it easy for clients to grab what they need during class.

Do you also sell products in your yoga studio? The modular design of our shelving systems allows for easy adjustments to accommodate changes in your product range, making them outstanding retail display shelves. Adjusting the shelving to an update of your yoga studio equipment or a change in product range requires just a few simple steps - time and cost-efficient.

Display Your Yoga Equipment with Height-Adjustable Shelves and Support Bars

Versatility is key in a dynamic yoga studio. Our shelves and support barsare height-adjustablein 5 cm (1.97 inches) increments, enabling easy display of products of different heights. From water bottles to yoga blocks, our flexible system ensures that everything has its place and is presented attractively.

At the same time, robust materials ensure a high carrying capacity, even with intensive use. With their timeless design, the shelves blend harmoniously into any yoga studio interior and emphasize the relaxed atmosphere.

Open Organizer box

The open organizer box offers a fantastic solution for displaying or storing yoga mats. It is flexible and can be moved around within the shelf or removed if required.

Due to its generous capacity, it is also excellent for storing yoga straps, towels, or other accessories. With its modern design, it blends seamlessly into your yoga studio furniture and ensures neat and stylish organization.
