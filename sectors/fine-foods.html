Fixtures for Farm Shops, Deli Stores and Fine Foods

Attractive Shop Fittings for Delicatessens, Farm Shops, and Health Food Stores

Due to the increasing popularity of high-quality organic food and fine deli products, store equipment and fittings are gaining importance: More and more stores are opening in this sector these days, making it increasingly necessary to stand out. Attractive product presentation and appealing store fittings play a crucial role in the sale of delicatessen products. Modern customers appreciate both fresh, high-quality products and a special shopping experience, allowing them to feel completely at ease in your store.

Beyond sophisticated store design, the flexibility of your concept is also essential: delicatessen store equipment should always be adaptable to seasonal changes. That way, changing product ranges can be effectively displayed, from fruit and vegetables to international delicacies. At Mandai Design, you will find sophisticated deli store furnishings and versatile shopfitting systems for tasteful store designs.

Shelving system Ceres

Perfect for displaying organic produce and beverages

Available in various widths and heights, Ceres offers plenty of space for food, fruit, or delicatessen. Depending on the range and season, you can adjust the height of the shelves without tools. The chalkboard visually highlights the regional origin of your products and can be written on with standard chalk or chalk markers.

As a modular system, Ceres is well-suited for presenting organic products and deli foods – the store equipment is easily converted and extended with additional shelving modules. Additionally, this furnishing system lacks its back panel, creating stylish looks with its straightforward, timeless design.

Shelving system Addison

Flexible set-up of farm shops

While the assortments and products of farm shops change quickly, you can prepare yourself with our Addison sales shelves. Its adaptive, customizable shelving modulespresent your products in style. This versatility makes Addison suitable for the presentation of organic foods, coffee and tea, or delicatessen. This store equipment can be further varied with flexible accessories and modular carriers, fashioning creative shelving systemsin your store.

Whether you want to expand your product range or run seasonal sales promotions, the shelving units in the Addison range offer you the flexibility you are looking for. Addison can also be set up without wall mounting, making it a freestanding room divider.

Wine Rack Addison

Perfect for wine bars, wine cellars or restaurants

Wooden shelves offer plenty of spaceto present wine bottles, spirits, or the corresponding glasses. The shelves are hooked into the vertical steel frame and can be adjusted in height every 5 cm, allowing you to make flexible changes as required.

Thanks to strong shelf consoles, the Addison wine rack can easily carry heavy goods such as wine bottles (up to 30 kg per shelf). In addition, a presentation box with diagonal dividers provides excellent storage for bottles and doubles as a stylish eye-catcher.

Gondola Shelving

Perfect for Delicatessen Stores, Organic Food Shops & Farm Shops

Gondola shelving is the ideal solution for displaying delicatessen products, organic foods and regional specialties in the center of your retail space. The double-sided design creates additional display space while allowing different product categories—such as oils, vinegars, spices, jams, or seasonal products—to be clearly organized and attractively presented.

At the same time, gondola shelving encourages a natural customer flow and allows shoppers to explore your assortment from different angles. Thanks to its modular design, the shelving can be easily adapted to new products or changing seasonal displays at any time. The result is a premium merchandise presentation that enhances the character of delicatessen stores, farm shops, and organic food retailers while creating a clear, welcoming shopping experience.

Organic-,Farm-, and Deli Store Equipment from Mandai Design

Mandai Design is your designer and manufacturer of high-quality modular retail fixtures that allow for aesthetic and functional store designs. Our sales shelves offer versatile solutions for various sectors and concepts: Present goods such as delicatessen with our store equipment and rely on quality and proven functionality!

Your benefits with Mandai Design at a glance:

  • Flexible and versatile systems for customized interior designs
  • Robust and durable materials with precise workmanship
  • Manufactured in our own factory directly in Germany for high quality standards
  • Clear, minimalist designs that are flexible and functional
  • Modular design for easy adaptation to changing product ranges
  • Simple assembly due to intelligent design
  • Planning support to make the best and most stylish use of space

Our store fixtures can be put together completely individually from various modules, giving you the chance to choose exactly the retail display shelves, tables, and counters that match your design ideas and functional requirements. Custom-made deli store equipment is also an option: We support you from the initial concept design to the final realization with our comprehensive know-how.

Adjustments to Seasonal Changes

In delicatessens as well as in organic food shops, the products usually change periodically – fresh fruit and vegetables, regional specialties, or new delicacies enrich the range depending on the season. Our modular deli store equipment is an excellent solution for flexible adaptation to these changes: It enables the flexible arrangement and presentation of your products in just a few simple steps. This means you always have space for the latest offers and seasonal delicatessen highlights, without having to spend time and money reorganizing the store fittings.

Make the Most of Your Space and Sell More

With Mandai Design’s well-thought-out store fittings, you can use your available space efficiently and present products in an immediately eye-catching way. Our systems are designed to also make use of the central space in your store due to freestanding store fixtures. This helps maintain freedom of movement for your customers. This clever use of space increases the attractive display of delicatessen products, encouraging more impulse purchases and promoting the flow of goods.

Stylish Presentation of High-Quality Delicacies

Aesthetic presentation is particularly important when it comes to delicatessen products. In addition to high product quality, customers expect a stylish ambience that emphasizes the value of the delicacies. Our timeless designs create a harmonious look and draw attention to the goods on display. Whether fine wines, handmade chocolates, or international delicacies - Mandai Design’s store equipment creates an elegant and appealing setting for organic and fine foods.
