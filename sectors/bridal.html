Retail fixtures for bridal salons

For your bridal shop to succeed in the wedding fashion business, the right shop fittings - retail shelving, racks, rails, and hangers - are a must. Dress racks and rails

Racks for elegant and reliable presentation

The build quality of our racks meets heavy-duty industrial standards without compromising on style. This makes our clothing rails especially suitable for wedding shops. Our classic Como, the distinctive Kansas, or the unique Jade all offer various hanging heights and customized finishes. They can also be used to create sections for bridesmaid dresses or for partitioning.

Clothing Rail System Ontario

Secure fashion wall display

A bridal shop requires reliable clothing rails to hang heavy, wide, and a variety of dresses. We developed our wall rail system, Ontario, with these needs in mind - height, weight, and construction to suit the fashion industry.

The unobtrusive design makes it the perfect clothing rail, with valuable shelf space on top for accessories, veils, and shoes.

Additional retail furniture

Impeccable for the salon and events

For complete shop fittings, add display tables or cubes to provide space for hair accessories, shoes, jewelry, and floral arrangements.

The cubes are especially practical when nested together or standing separately - centrally or along walls, alone or combined with racks. They also work well as bases for mannequins or in your shop window.
