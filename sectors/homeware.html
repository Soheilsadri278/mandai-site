If you want to present homeware and accessories elegantly and stylishly, Addison is the perfect shop shelving system. A continuously perforated grid offers flexible equipping with all common display fittings, while presentation boxes make staging your products easy.

Minimalist and deliberately unobtrusive

For the stylish presentation of homeware and decorative items in the salesroom, you need timeless and reserved shop furnishings. The many different products in the home area alone create a lively product image in the store - the furniture should hold back and let your products shine.

Strong by design, Addison can also be used as a free-standing shelf without wall mounting when utilizing the provided diagonal connectors - for example as a stylish room divider with our wine rack, or as an inviting corner display.

Retail fixtures for homeware stores - timeless and reserved

Addison - flexible perforated grid

A continuously perforated grid offers flexible equipping with all common display fittings. Presentation boxes, shelves and rails can be repositioned at any time - ideal for changing homeware ranges.

Ceres - light and airy

Minimalist and deliberately unobtrusive, shelving system Ceres provides an understated stage for your decorations. Shelves and rails hook into the wall columns without tools.

Free-standing room dividers

Thanks to diagonal connectors, Addison works without wall mounting - perfect for dividing larger homeware stores into themed areas.
