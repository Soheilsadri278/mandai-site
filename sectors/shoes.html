Ceres Shelving System

Our Ceres shelving system is a versatile solution that adapts perfectly to the needs of a modern shoe retailer. The individual system modules connect seamlessly with one another, allowing your displays to evolve alongside your expanding product range. Thanks to a 5-centimeter grid, each shoe display shelf height can be easily adjusted, making it easy to create the ideal presentation for everything from sneakers to ankle boots.

Whether you choose one of our preconfigured setups or put together your own custom combination, furnishing your shoe store with pieces from the Ceres line is a long-term investment in flexibility, quality, and a cohesive shopping experience. Not sure which option is right for your store?

Showcase Product Highlights with Display Pedestals

Display pedestals are versatile design elements that help you showcase standout pieces from your latest collection with style. Our display pedestals are a flexible way to add creative flair to your shoe store. They are perfect for spotlighting standout pieces that deserve extra attention. Whether placed in the shop window or the center of your store, display pedestals are easy to adapt to your product lineup and overall store concept.
