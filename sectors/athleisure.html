Retail Fixtures for Athleisure Stores

Dynamic and Flexible Store Design for Athleisure Stores

Every athleisure store has unique requirements, depending on the products it sells and its target audience. Whether it’s a specialized shoe store, a sports apparel shop, or a retailer of sports equipment and accessories, each concept demands tailored fixtures. That’s why a well-thought-out and adaptable store design is essential - not only for showcasing products attractively but also for seamlessly adjusting to seasonal changes and new collections. Mandai Design offers modular store fixture systems that allow you to create a flexible and visually striking retail environment, ensuring your merchandise always takes center stage.

Shelving System Ceres

The Ceres shelving system is the perfect solution for athleisure stores that value flexible and thoughtful product presentation. Thanks to its modular design, you enjoy maximum flexibility. The individual modules can be combined as needed, and the shelves and clothing bars can be adjusted in 5 cm (1.97 inches) increments for a customized setup. Select from one of our pre-configured styles below, or contact us for a custom solution.

More Retail Fixtures for Athleisure Stores

Clothing Racks

Clothing racks are a must-have for any athleisure store. They not only keep sportswear organized but also provide a stylish way to showcase bestsellers and new collections at the heart of your store. We offer a wide range of clothing racks in various designs, sizes, and materials to suit any retail space. Whether you prefer a minimalist, functional, or statement-making look, our solutions help you present your products in the best possible way.

Discover Clothing racks

Gondola Shelving

Gondola shelving creates additional retail space in the center of your store while providing an organized, double-sided product display. Sportswear, footwear, and accessories can be clearly arranged and flexibly combined, allowing customers to explore different product categories at a glance. Thanks to the modular design, the shelving can be easily adapted to new collections or seasonal assortments at any time.

Discover Gondola Shelving

Display Pedestals

Display pedestals add depth and dimension to your store design, making them a great addition to your store fixtures for showcasing sports products. Whether used individually as striking focal points or arranged together for a dynamic display, they allow you to create impactful product presentations with ease. Perfect for the center of your store or window displays, these pedestals draw attention to featured items and enhance the overall shopping experience.

Discover display pedestals

Display Tables

Display tables offer a versatile and stylish way to showcase products in an athleisure store. With ample space for neatly arranging sports shoes, accessories, and folded clothing, they help create an inviting and organized shopping environment. Our collection features a variety of shapes and sizes - from round and rectangular to square - designed to fit your specific needs.

Discover display tables

Shop Counters

The shop counter plays a central role in every athleisure store - it’s where customers seek advice, finalize their purchases, and engage with your brand. A well-designed and functional counter not only streamlines transactions but also enhances the overall shopping experience. With a wide selection of counters available in various colors, shapes, and sizes, we offer tailored solutions to seamlessly integrate into your store’s design.
