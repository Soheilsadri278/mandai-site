Clear presentation for ever-changing ranges

Our Ontario clothing rail system features a sleek, minimalist design that seamlessly fits into a wide range of store concepts. It offers generous hanging space, complemented by a glass shelf - perfect for showcasing toys, folded clothes or other children's accessories.

Lugano gondola shelving helps create clear and attractive product presentation within the store. The open, freestanding design keeps products visible and easily accessible from multiple sides - ideal for children's clothing, toys, gifts and changing seasonal ranges.
