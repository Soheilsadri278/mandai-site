Other Fixtures for Kids Stores

From eye-catching display pedestal sets to clothing racks and sleek shop counters, we offer a wide range of stylish product display furniture for your childrens store.

Wall-mounted rails

Our Ontario clothing rail system features a sleek, minimalist design that seamlessly fits into a wide range of store concepts. It offers generous hanging space, complemented by a glass shelf, perfect for showcasing toys, folded clothes, or other children’s accessories. Optional wooden pedestals provide even more space to present kids’ shoes or additional products at the bottom.

Shop Ontario clothing rails

Gondola shelving

Lugano gondola shelving helps create clear and attractive product presentation within the store. The open, freestanding design keeps products visible and easily accessible from multiple sides, making it ideal for children’s clothing, toys, gifts, and changing seasonal collections. Height-adjustable shelf boards provide the flexibility to accommodate different product sizes and evolving assortments, supporting efficient and versatile merchandising throughout the retail space.

Free-standing racks

Showcase childrens clothing and accessories with our sleek racks and rails. Perfect for shop windows, central displays, or wall arrangements, they guide clients towards new combinations and related products.
