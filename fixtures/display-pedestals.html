About Display Pedestals from Takinta Design

Diverse sizes and finishes to suit your space

Our retail display pedestals can be purchased individually or as practical sets. Explore a wide range of shapes and sizes, ranging from sleek and slim designs to square cube tables, catering to your unique needs.

Select the perfect display pedestal to showcase your products, whether it’s bags, accessories, cosmetics, or decorative items. From shorter options to taller display stands, find the ideal fit for your space and merchandise. Customize further by choosing from an array of colors and finishes – classic white and anthracite for timeless elegance, rustic wood decors for a natural touch, or stay on-trend with a luxurious marble-like finish. Other materials, such as solid wood, are available on request.

Flexible product presentation

With our stylish display pedestals and cube tables, new and interesting product displays can be created anytime and without effort. The sets can be combined in any way you like and can be rearranged again and again. Stack the cubes on top of each other or nest them together, experimenting with different colours to create an eye-catching product display in your window or at the center of your store. Whether for a specific promotion, a change of collection or simply to refresh the look - with our display cubes, changes in the shop can be made quickly and easily.

Create eye-catching shop window displays

Display accessories, shoes, homeware and more on our stylish pedestals to spotlight new arrivals, sale highlights or curated collections. By playing with different heights, shapes and colours, you can build visually compelling scenes that stop passersby in their tracks. A well-designed shop window doesn’t just elevate your brand - it captures attention, increases footfall, and helps turn window-shoppers into customers.

FAQ: Display Pedestals and Cube Tables

Cube tables and pedestals are used for versatile and appealing product presentation in retail stores, boutiques, and salesrooms. They serve as a flexible alternative to traditional sales tables and allow products such as bags, shoes, accessories, or decorative items to be showcased.

The main advantages of cube tables and pedestals are their versatility and flexibility. They can be combined in any way or used individually, fit into any room concept, and are easy to transport.

The best material for cube tables is high-quality decorative chipboard, as used by Takinta Design. This surface is durable, easy to clean, and available in many appealing designs.

Display pedestals for product presentation from Takinta Design are extremely stable and suitable for daily use in retail. The precise workmanship and high-quality, melamine-coated chipboard ensure long-lasting stability, even with frequent rearrangement or intensive use.

Our cube tables and display pedestals are available in many practical sizes. The smallest size is suitable for individual presentation of smaller products, while the larger versions are ideal for bigger items as well as mannequins. All sizes are available individually or as practical sets and can be optimally adapted to your space requirements.

Pedestals are firmly glued together at the factory and delivered on a disposable pallet by a shipping company. They can be used immediately.

Display pedestals are usually closed and without storage space as free-standing presentation elements.

For optimal presentation, use multiple levels in height and depth, an odd number of pieces, and groups of three. This creates a sense of excitement and dynamic effect. Top-sellers and branded goods should always be showcased. Decorative cube tables support optimal product presentation, whether as a pedestal for mannequins or in combination with clothing racks for retail stores in the middle of the room.

We are happy to offer advice on your product presentation upon request. Our design and planning service includes detailed 2D and 3D drawings as well as professional support for optimal store design.
