Boutique Display Tables are Silent Sellers

Display tables play an important role in the store concept. An attractively designed table invites customers to stop, touch, and take a closer look at the goods – so they can experience the product directly and develop their desire to buy it. In addition, the retail display table offers excellent opportunities to highlight specific items or product groups. This makes it an indispensable table for special offers and a focal point for sales promotions. As silent sellers, they present newly arrived goods, act as a stopper, and extend the time the customer spends in the store.
