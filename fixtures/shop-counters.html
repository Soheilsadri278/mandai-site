FAQ: Retail Counters

A retail counter is the central piece of furniture in a store, where consultations take place and the purchasing process is completed. However, it serves not only as a work surface, but also as a visual flagship for your store. Ideally, its design conveys professionalism and emphasizes the character of the interior.

The height of a retail counter is based on ergonomic standards and should be comfortable for both customers and employees to use. At Mandai Design, our retail counters have a standard height of 92 to 95 cm. Since the retail counters are custom-made, dimensions and designs can be flexibly adapted. This means you get a solution that fits your space concept perfectly. Please contact us directly.

The ideal dimensions depend on the layout of the room and how you want to use the retail counter. Thanks to Mandai Design's custom manufacturing, the height, width, and depth can be tailored precisely to your requirements. This allows the counter to blend harmoniously into your store while also meeting functional requirements.

The store counter should be positioned where it is clearly visible to customers and complements the layout of the store. A good location is often in the entrance or exit area, as this is where purchases are made. It is important that the counter supports the overall impression of the store and complements it harmoniously.

When planning a checkout counter, its functionality and appeal are crucial. Ideally, surfaces should be easy to clean and offer sufficient space for technology and various equipment. Simultaneously, the retail counter should serve as an elegant design element and emphasize your business concept.
