FAQ: Clothing Racks

A clothing or garment rack is a freestanding fixture used to present apparel in an organized, easy-to-scan way. In retail, it lets you display items within easy reach, refresh assortments seasonally, and structure the sales floor.

Width varies by model and intended use. In retail, widths between 60 cm and 125 cm are common. Narrow racks from 60 cm work well for smaller areas, alcoves, or showcasing selected highlights. Wider models measuring 120 to 125 cm, on the other hand, offer enough space to display entire collections side by side.

The minimum height of a clothing rack depends on the type of clothing to be displayed. For pants or shirts, a lower height of around 130 cm is sufficient, while for long dresses or coats, taller racks of 150 to 170 cm and above are more appropriate.

Mandai Design clothing racks are made of high-quality steel and are exceptionally robust. Built for daily use in retail, our racks stay true to shape and tip-resistant even when fully loaded with jackets, pants, or accessories.

The number of items of clothing that can fit on a garment rack depends on the width of the rack and the type of clothing. A 60 cm wide rack can hold around 15 to 20 hangers with lighter fabrics, or 8 to 10 hangers with thicker garments. A 120 cm wide rack can hold twice as many items.

Base your selection on the store area, product range, and desired presentation style. Also consider how well it can be combined with other store fixtures and whether it offers the flexibility needed for seasonal changes. At Mandai Design, we offer various series for different room sizes and design requirements.

The design of your clothing rack should complement the atmosphere of your store. Mandai Design offers a range of different design lines for this purpose. The Como series impresses with its timeless elegance in classic black or white. The Kansas series combines metal frames with decorative surfaces such as wood or stone looks, creating a cozy, elegant accent.

Clothing racks should be made of high-quality, durable materials that can withstand everyday wear and tear and also look good. At Mandai Design, we primarily use powder-coated steel and melamine-coated wood-based materials. Most of the joints are firmly welded together, offering optimal stability and an attractive design.

Ideally, a clothing rack should be placed in the center of the room, along the side aisles, or in the entrance area, depending on which products you want to highlight. It is important to ensure that there is enough room to move around and that the products are easily accessible. In shop windows, racks attract additional attention and spark customers' curiosity.

A clothing rack can be used to present clothing in a clear, accessible, and visually appealing way. With a clothing rack, collections can be sorted by color, size, or style. At the same time, you can highlight seasonal trends and emphasize new collections or promotional items.

Yes, mid-floor clothing racks are ideal for visually separating different areas in the salesroom. They structure the room and at the same time draw attention to selected goods.

A good clothing rack is stable, durable, and versatile. It combines functionality with an attractive design that blends harmoniously into the store concept. Additionally, it should be easy to handle and quick to move around as needed.
