Modular Gondola Shelving

Gondola shelving is one of the most versatile merchandising solutions for retail stores. Freestanding gondola shelves create additional display space while helping organize products throughout the sales floor. Accessible from multiple sides, they make it easy to highlight merchandise and encourage product visibility. Adjustable shelves allow the system to accommodate different product sizes and changing assortments, making display gondolas a flexible solution for a wide range of retail environments.

Gondola Shelving Lugano

Open, modern design.

Its space-efficient design is particularly well suited to modern retail environments where product visibility and efficient use of floor space are key considerations.

FAQ: Gondola Retail Shelving

Gondola shelving is a freestanding retail fixture that can be positioned throughout the sales floor to create additional display space wherever it is needed.

Because gondola shelves can be accessed from multiple sides, they are ideal for creating central floor displays, product islands, promotional areas, and well-organized store layouts. They help retailers maximize floor space, improve product visibility, and guide customers through the store more effectively.

Unlike traditional wall shelving, gondola shelving offers greater flexibility when planning and organizing retail environments. With a variety of sizes, configurations, and finishes available, Mandai Design gondola shelving systems can be adapted to a wide range of products and store concepts, from small boutiques and specialty stores to larger retail environments.

Gondola shelving comes in a variety of configurations to suit different store layouts and merchandising needs. Depending on the products being showcased, retailers may choose between single-sided and double-sided display options.

Many modern gondola shelving systems are modular, allowing them to be adapted as requirements change. This makes it easy to reorganize and adjust sales floors over time, making gondola shelving a long-term solution for stores that regularly update their assortments and product displays.

Gondola shelving is used to create additional merchandising space throughout the sales floor. As freestanding fixtures, gondola shelves make effective use of central floor areas while keeping products organized and easily accessible from multiple sides.

Depending on the configuration, retail gondolas can accommodate hanging apparel, folded garments, accessories, cosmetics, home goods, and many other product categories. Different shelving options and display fittings support a variety of presentation styles, making gondola shelving suitable for a wide range of stores and retail concepts.

Mandai Design gondola shelving is designed to adapt to changing products and store requirements. Shelves and display fittings can be added, rearranged, or replaced as needed, while shelf heights can be adjusted in 50 mm (1.97") increments to suit different products and presentation styles. This flexibility makes it easy to update product displays, introduce new collections, or adapt layouts over time without replacing entire fixtures.

An additional advantage is that Lugano gondola shelving is compatible with the Ceres and Cetus retail shelving systems, providing greater freedom when creating cohesive store layouts.

The ideal height for gondola shelving depends on your product range, available floor space, and the shopping experience you want to create. For most retail environments, a height that provides sufficient display space while maintaining clear visibility across the sales floor is often the best solution. In many stores, a height of 155 cm (61") has proven to be a practical choice.

Lower configurations are particularly well suited to open shop concepts, as they allow for clearer views across the sales floor and create a more open feel throughout the store. Taller versions provide additional display space and are often a practical choice for stores with larger assortments or limited floor space.

Mandai Design gondola shelving is designed for everyday use in retail environments and is suitable for a wide range of product categories. The maximum load capacity of a gondola shelving unit depends on its configuration and intended use.

Choosing the right gondola shelving starts with the products you want to display. Fashion rails are ideal for hanging apparel, while slanted shelves help showcase books, magazines and more in a front-facing position. Straight shelves are well suited to folded clothing, cosmetics, fragrances, and many other product categories.

It is also important to consider how easily the system can adapt over time. Modular retail gondolas can be reconfigured as assortments change, making it easier to update product displays and store layouts without investing in entirely new store fixtures.
