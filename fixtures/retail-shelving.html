Modules for Sales Shelves

Our shelving systems and wall shelves can be fitted with various elements and shelf accessories. Their positions can be changed at any time without tools. Find out more about the different modules for your individual sales shelf:

Straight shelf boards

Choose from different materials and thicknesses. Height adjustable every 5cm.

Angled shelf boards

Perfect for displaying heavier items and products laying flat.

Presentation box

Perfect for the stable presentation of heavier products. Can also be fitted with a glass shelf as shown here.

Storage box

Drawer cabinets that can be inserted into the bottom of the shelf and offers valuable storage space.

Pedestal

Finish your shelf with this stylish base which provides additional room to present your products.

Shelf accessories

Front arms, fashion rails and more to present hanging items such as clothing.

If you are redesigning or modernizing a store, you often do not need a complete overhaul of your store fixtures. It is often sufficient to customize your store space with selected shelving units. Retail Display Shelves for Flexible Set-up

Whether it’s a store, showroom or pop-up store, the design of sales areas plays a major role in store design. This makes it all the more important to select the right store shelving during the planning stage. Open sales shelves without a back wall look lighter and avoid the typical supermarket feeling in the sales room. Another advantage is the uncomplicated design. Additional elements for the store shelving can be added flexibly and quickly when changing and expanding the product range. At Mandai Design, you will also find matching complementary store fixtures such as display tables for retail stores and retail counters.

Maximize the Sales Area with Individual Retail Shelves

With rising prices for rented commercial premises, the desire for effectively utilized retail space is increasing. Our slim and linear shelving units offer a good solution here. The Addison retail shelving unit, for example, can also be installed without wall mounting. This ensures effective use of the central space without appearing bulky. As many shelves can be combined with each other, a variety of sales islands can be created. Our sales shelves are quickly changed and create a new image in the middle room.

Stylish Store Shelving for Many Sectors

FAQ: Retail Display Shelves

Retail display shelves are central elements of store fixtures and are used to display products and merchandise. They structure the sales area, provide storage space, and support appealing merchandise presentation. At the same time, they influence the ambience and contribute to brand impact.

The optimal height depends on the area of application and the spatial conditions. If sales shelves are placed directly in front of a wall, common heights are between 230 and 260 cm. This ensures that products are clearly visible and easily reachable for customers.

The depth depends on the type of goods. For cosmetics or jewelry, 30 to 40 cm is usually sufficient, while for larger products such as homeware or clothing, deeper shelves of around 50 cm may be more appropriate. It is important to maintain a good balance between storage space and freedom of movement in the store.
