Design as a success factor in retail

Most customers are guided by emotions when making a purchase. They want to be positively surprised and expect a special shopping experience when entering a shop. In this respect, the design of a shop interior plays a major role in successfully attracting customers. An interior beautiful design is somewhat more cost-intensive to purchase, but more economical in the long run.
